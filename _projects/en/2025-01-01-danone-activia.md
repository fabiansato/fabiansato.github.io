AEM development for Danone, Danonino and Activia, focused on reusable components and brand experiences.

The documented experience includes components for Danonino Mexico, Danone US and Activia US, and technical leadership on the Activia US-EN implementation within Danone France’s digital ecosystem.

The work covered AEM Cloud architecture, templates, content fragment modelling and frontend pipelines. It also included coordination across teams in France, Brazil, Mexico and Argentina, and integrations with SmartMedia, Bazaarvoice and GA4.

## Technologies

AEM Cloud, HTL, Sling Models, React, GA4.
