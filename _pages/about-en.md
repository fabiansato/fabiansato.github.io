People call me Sato. I'm Fabian Gonzalez, a full-stack developer specializing in Adobe Experience Manager and frontend. I enjoy the meeting point between an idea, an interface and the code that brings everything together.

I have more than ten years of experience building websites, campaigns and digital platforms. I've worked on projects for LATAM, the United States and Canada, alongside distributed teams and across design, content and technology.

My background in graphic design gave me a habit: paying attention to how the things we build feel. Development added another: thinking about the person who will use, edit and maintain them next. A clear component, a fast page and a thoughtful explanation can make a real difference.

This site brings together projects, experiments and lessons. Not everything starts as a big project: sometimes a useful tool grows out of a repetitive task, a question or a strong urge to understand something.

**Everyone can learn to code.** Sharing what I learn is my way of keeping that idea moving.
